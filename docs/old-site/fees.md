## Fees
### Car Finance Claims
checkfinance.co.uk does not charge you for using our service and acts solely as an introducer. If you choose to proceed with a claim, you will enter into a separate agreement with Consultation Claims Limited, who are authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority to provide claims management services and will handle your claim.

There are no upfront costs to you. A fee will only be payable if your claim is successful. Fees are charged in bands depending on the amount of redress or refund received. All fees quoted are exclusive of VAT, which will be added at the prevailing rate.

### Cancelling
If you proceed with Consultation Claims Limited, you have a 14-day cooling-off period starting from the date you sign or e-sign their Letter of Authority and/or Terms of Engagement. During this period, you may cancel without charge.

If you cancel your agreement after the 14-day cooling-off period has ended, cancellation fees may apply in accordance with Consultation Claims Limited’s Terms of Engagement.
