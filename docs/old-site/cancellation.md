## Cancellation
### Notice of Right to Cancel
Check Finance acts an introducer to certain claim handling partners. It does not provide claim handling services.

If you have signed a Letter of Authority and/or Terms of Engagement with one of our claim handling partners, you must cancel with them directly. You have a 14-day cooling-off period during which you may cancel your claim without charge.

If you have signed a Letter of Authority and/or Terms of Engagement with Consultation Claims Limited., please contact them directly here: https://consultationclaims.co.uk/cancellation/
